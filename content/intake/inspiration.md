# References to emulate

Chosen direction (Rudolf approved "proceed with your recommendation"). Study these for
**structure** and **restraint**, not to copy visually.

## The three to copy structure from
1. **Andrej Karpathy** — karpathy.ai — restraint; lets the work speak, almost no chrome.
2. **Andy Matuschak** — andymatuschak.org · **Maggie Appleton** — maggieappleton.com — the working-notes / backlinks "digital garden" (model for our Notes page).
3. **Simon Willison** — simonwillison.net — the "learn in public" / TIL cadence; plain, prolific, effective.

## Also worth a look
- AI/ML writing: Chris Olah (colah.github.io), Lilian Weng (lilianweng.github.io), Jay Alammar (jalammar.github.io)
- Minimalist legends: Fabrice Bellard (bellard.org), antirez (antirez.com), Paul Graham (paulgraham.com)
- Modern dev craft: Lee Robinson (leerob.io), Josh Comeau (joshwcomeau.com), Rauno Freiberg (rauno.me), Guillermo Rauch (rauchg.com)

## What we're taking from them
- Karpathy: quiet hero, content-first, no flashy motion.
- Matuschak/Appleton: the Notes page as a linked garden with backlinks (already built).
- Willison: a steady, honest "what I'm learning/building" rhythm (the Now page + garden).
